# Founder walkthrough

Open [VICT Inspections](http://127.0.0.1:5221/). This is a fictional demo.

1. Select **Cold-chain compressor room**. Review the severity-labelled findings and evidence cards. Image references are clearly labelled placeholders.
2. Type a reason in **Reason for return**, then choose **Return for correction**. The status changes and your reason appears in Activity. There is one decision area and one history.
3. Open **Demo controls**, switch **View as** to Technician and choose **Start corrections**. Add a finding and labelled evidence, then **Submit for review**.
4. Switch back to Supervisor. Choose **Approve inspection**. The review is complete; no further approval button remains. Return to the queue to see the updated status.
5. In Demo controls, open **Operation coverage & test console** to inspect delayed, failed, unavailable, denied and conflicting operation cases. These are console cases; ordinary product approval follows its actual runtime result. If a product operation reports a stale record, **Reload inspection** reads its current state.
6. Choose **Empty queue** or **Long content** under **Records (simulated)** and reset to inspect those layouts. Choose **Submitted inspections** and reset to restore the ordinary journey. Storage is labelled **Simulated** or **Saved locally**. Saved records persist when settings change.
7. To edit presentation, open **Edit presentation**, select the title, open **Style**, change Text size with normal typing, leave the field and Save. Reopen detail on this same browser origin: the actual product uses your saved presentation. The queue remains editable in canonical source; the browser editor in this slice edits detail.
